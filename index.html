A car already part of motorsport history

The Hypercar that has dominated the global endurance scene

The 499P has already written its name into Ferrari's racing heritage, bringing the Prancing Horse back to the top tier of endurance racing since 2023 with seven overall victories and three consecutive wins at the 24 Hours of Le Mans.

At the heart of the project remains the hybrid powertrain: a centrally mounted twin‑turbo V6 engine paired with an electric motor on the front axle, working together to ensure consistent performance and energy efficiency throughout each race.

V6 ICE ENGINE • 500 kW • 800V • 4WD

V6 ICE ENGINE

The internal combustion engine delivers a regulation-limited maximum output of 500 kW (680 hp). Derived from Ferrari's road‑going V6 family, it features tailored components and substantial weight optimization.

ERS SYSTEM

The Energy Recovery System delivers up to 200 kW (272 hp). Energy recovered under braking is stored in an 800V battery developed using Formula 1 expertise.

7-SPEED GEARBOX

Power delivery is handled by a seven‑speed sequential gearbox engineered to withstand the extreme demands of endurance competition.

LOAD-BEARING ENGINE

Unlike the 296 GT3, the V6 in the 499P is fully load‑bearing, contributing structurally to the car's rigidity. The internal combustion unit comprises approximately 500 components.

Shaped by the Wind

Balanced Performance

Recent updates include targeted interventions to control rear brake temperatures and refine aerodynamic stability. Two additional nose elements help optimize airflow distribution with minimal impact on outright lap time.

Adjustable Rear Wing

A regulation‑compliant adjustable aerodynamic device is integrated into the rear wing, allowing engineers to fine‑tune load distribution between the front and rear axles.

Heat Management

Large vents behind the front wheels discharge hot air generated by the braking system. Development included simulations, wind tunnel work, simulator sessions and track testing over more than 22 months.

ERS Integration

The electric unit in the 499P operates either independently or in tandem with the front brake discs to decelerate the car during braking. During acceleration, it enables the activation of the 4WD system.
