RACING SUPREMACY

CONQUERING THE CIRCUIT DE LA SARTHE

Fifty years after its last official factory appearance in the top endurance category, Ferrari returned to the 24 Hours of Le Mans and triumphed in historic fashion with the 499P.

At the core of the project is an ultra-compact 120° twin-turbo V6 engine derived from the 296 GT3, engineered directly as a structural chassis member. Combined with an electric motor-generator unit on the front axle, the 499P achieves all-wheel drive with seamless torque vectoring.

LMH ARCHITECTURE

ENGINEERING LE MANS PERFECTION

Every subsystem was forged to withstand 24 hours of non-stop, maximum-attack racing at speeds exceeding 340 km/h down the Mulsanne straight.

120° V6 Engine

Mounted centrally with twin turbos nestled inside the vee for ultra-low center of gravity and packaging efficiency.

Front Electric Axle

A 200 kW (272 hp) front electric motor connected through a differential, disengaged below 190 km/h by FIA regulations.

Brake-by-Wire

Electronic kinetic energy harvesting captures mega-joules of braking power on every deceleration zone.

Xtrac 7-Speed Gearbox

Transverse sequential 7-speed transmission integrated within the rear suspension structure.
