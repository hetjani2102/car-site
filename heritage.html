THE MARANELLO CHRONICLE

DEFINING MOMENTS IN AUTOMOTIVE HISTORY

Every era produced an automotive icon that redefined speed, engineering, and passion.

1947

The First Ferrari: 125 S

Enzo Ferrari drives the 125 S out through the historic factory gates on Via Abetone Inferiore in Maranello, equipped with a 1.5-liter Colombo V12 engine.

1962

Ferrari 250 GTO

Regarded as the greatest sports car of all time, winning three consecutive FIA GT World Championships and becoming the undisputed holy grail of collectors.

1967

Daytona 1-2-3 Historic Finish

Ferrari executes an unforgettable side-by-side photo finish sweep at the 24 Hours of Daytona with the 330 P3/4, 330 P4, and 412 P on American soil.

1987

The F40: Enzo's Final Masterpiece

To celebrate the 40th anniversary, Ferrari unveils the twin-turbo carbon-Kevlar F40—the first production street car to break the 200 mph (322 km/h) barrier.

2000-04

The Golden Formula 1 Dynasty

Michael Schumacher and Scuderia Ferrari dominate the pinnacle of motorsport, capturing 5 consecutive Drivers' and 6 consecutive Constructors' World Titles.

2023-25

Le Mans Centenary Return & Triplet

Ferrari returns to the top hypercar class after 50 years and wins the 24 Hours of Le Mans three years in a row with the 499P hybrid hypercar.
