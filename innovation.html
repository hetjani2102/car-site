INNOVATION & TECHNOLOGY

Transforming track-proven Formula 1 and Le Mans engineering into transcendent road car dynamics.

HIGH-VOLTAGE HYBRID

HYBRID POWERTRAINS

From the triple-motor 1,000 CV architecture of the SF90 to the 120° turbo V6 hybrid in the 296 and 499P, Ferrari combines internal combustion soul with immediate electric torque fill. An 800V battery system delivers lightning discharge rates with zero turbo lag.

ACTIVE AERODYNAMICS

SHAPING THE AIRFLOW

Ferrari aerodynamics are invisible works of art. Rather than relying solely on aggressive wings, our cars utilize active rear flaps, front S-Ducts, and underbody Venturi tunnels to generate up to 500 kg of downforce without creating turbulence or excessive drag.

VEHICLE DYNAMICS

SIDE SLIP CONTROL 8.0 (SSC)

The electronic brain of every modern Ferrari. Using advanced six-way chassis sensors and predictive algorithmic telemetry, SSC estimates tire contact patch grip in real time, synchronizing the electronic differential (E-Diff), traction control (F1-Trac), and magnetorheological dampers.
